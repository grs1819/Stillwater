4 tiles: pebbles · 10: boulders · 18: plants

Outdoors: select Outdoors (7) and click dry ground for a lean-to. Click it again for a tent, then a glamping shelter. Adjacent camp tiles merge into one larger shelter. Choose Campfire, Seating, Picnic table, or Lantern to furnish your campsite yourself. Each piece occupies one dry ground tile. Role (8) assigns a purpose to existing buildings. Right-click removes; Undo restores.

Terrain: select Terrain (5), then Raise, Lower, or Level. Level samples a height on the first tap, then applies it. Keep structures on level clearings and leave water at its original height. Undo restores terrain too.

Build: click roofs to grow up, walls to grow out. Alt-click to repaint. Right-click to remove.

Touch: drag one finger to orbit and tilt. Drag two fingers to pan; pinch to zoom. Tap a tile to use the selected tool immediately. Hold still briefly for quick actions, including Undo if a tap was a mistake. Dragging and two-finger camera gestures never place blocks.

Explore: middle-drag to orbit and tilt, Shift + middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Q / E rotate; R recenters.

Roofing: choose a roof color. Cottages have natural moss over a colored roof; log cabins gain weathered ribbed metal; timber lodges gain polished standing-seam panels. Two stories or three rooms create a log cabin; four stories or ten rooms create a timber lodge.

Lookouts: stack at least three floors with a narrow one- or two-room summit to create panoramic windows, a telescope, map table, and woodburning stove. Add height to move the lookout upward.

Shape: connected cottages share sloped roofs. Build a taller neighbor to turn an exposed lower roof into a railed terrace. Raised entrances gain steps automatically.

Connect: bridge two upper walls for an archway. Choose Porch (2) on ground for a porch or terrace, or click an upper wall for a balcony. Adjacent porches join and match the attached building’s timber and roof.

Water: select Water (4), then Stream, River, or Lake. Click ground to add tiles; connected bodies gain detail as they grow. Cottages stand on stilts and paths become footbridges. Water can also be added beneath existing cottages and paths. With Water selected, right-click removes water only. Remove trees before adding water.

Village life: three cottages welcome residents. Connect at least three path tiles near a home to invite cyclists. Paths and footbridges give everyone safe routes around town.

Shortcuts: 1–9 choose tools; B opens the menu; Escape closes it; Ctrl / Cmd + Z undoes.
